Design brief

Nirman Constructions — a site notebook that syncs

What I read in your code

Six modules behind one permission-filtered tile grid; offline queue with DRAFT / PENDING / CONFLICT states; a theme written for sunlight — ink #14181D, terracotta signal #C2410C, 48px targets, mono digits, Indian number format.

The aesthetic

A surveyor's field book: warm paper, hand-inked rules and margin notes in Kalam — but every number, label and button stays IBM Plex at full contrast. Charm on the chrome, hard legibility on the data. Your existing palette is untouched.

The one structural change

Home becomes Today — what is unmarked, what is waiting on you, what this device has not sent — with a five-item bottom bar on phone and a left rail on desk. The tile grid survives as the "All screens" sheet, so nothing becomes unreachable.

Six registers, one evening's habit

Each one replaces a book that already exists on your site. Nothing here asks a supervisor to learn a new job.

MUSTER
Attendance & wages

One tap per worker, hours pre-filled from the standard shift, wage frozen the moment an engineer verifies it.

STORE
Material in & out

Receipts against the PO, issues against the work item, and a stock figure that matches the godown at closing time.

CASH
Expenses & approvals

Photograph the bill at the shop. It reaches the accountant with the site, the head and the amount already on it.

DIARY
Daily progress report

Six steps, resumable, photos attached. Signed off by the engineer, and the missing dates are named rather than counted.

BOQ
Progress against contract

Import the NIT, and completion is measured by value — nine cheap lines finished is not ninety per cent of a job.

OFFLINE
Built for basements

Every entry is written on the phone first with its own id, so a retry can never double-count it. It goes out by itself when there is a signal.
